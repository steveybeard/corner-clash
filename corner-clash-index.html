Corner Clash

Tap a piece to select it, then tap a green square to place it. Or press and hold a piece and drag it onto the board.

Corner Clash — Standard Rules

  • Each player has 21 pieces.
  • On your first turn, your piece must cover your own starting corner.
  • Every later piece must touch at least one of your own pieces corner-to-corner.
  • Your own pieces may never touch edge-to-edge.
  • Different colours may touch by edges or corners.
  • Pieces cannot overlap and placed pieces cannot be moved.
  • Pieces may be rotated or reflected before placement.
  • If you have a legal move, you must make one. If you have no legal move, you pass.
  • The game ends when all players are blocked or have no pieces left.
  • Scoring: unplayed squares are −1 each. Playing all 21 pieces earns +15. Finishing with the one-square piece adds +5.
  • Highest score wins.

Starting order: Blue, Yellow, Red, Green.
